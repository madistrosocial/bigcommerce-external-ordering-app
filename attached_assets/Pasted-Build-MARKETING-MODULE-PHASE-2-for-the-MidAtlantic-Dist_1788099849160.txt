==================================================
SENDING SAFETY
==================================================

Campaign sending must be designed safely.

Requirements:

- Prevent accidental duplicate sends.
- Prevent the same campaign from being started twice.
- Track send attempts.
- Track failures.
- Support retry handling where safe.
- Do not resend successfully delivered messages during retries.
- Log meaningful errors without exposing sensitive credentials.

Use idempotent processing where possible.

MODULE 7: UNSUBSCRIBE AND SUPPRESSION
==================================================

Build proper marketing suppression handling.

Requirements:

Customers must be excluded from marketing campaigns when:
