ERP Architecture Proposal Update
Warehouse Strategy & Fulfillment Domain Revision

This update supersedes any previous assumption that SalesCore's Inventory module is intended to replace SkuVault.

The long-term direction of SalesCore is not to become a Warehouse Management System (WMS). Instead, SalesCore should become the Business Operations ERP that orchestrates warehouse operations while integrating tightly with an external WMS.

Today that WMS is SkuVault, but the architecture should remain warehouse-agnostic so another WMS—or a future native SalesCore warehouse module—can be substituted without affecting the rest of the application.

New Architectural Principle
Warehouse-Agnostic ERP

SalesCore should never tightly couple its business logic to SkuVault.

Instead, all warehouse functionality should communicate through an internal Warehouse Integration Layer.

SalesCore ERP

↓

Warehouse Service

↓

SkuVault Adapter

↓

SkuVault API

If SalesCore eventually develops its own warehouse module, only the Warehouse Adapter changes.

CRM, Product 360, Reporting, Orders, Customer 360, Pricing, POS, and other ERP modules should never directly communicate with SkuVault.

Architectural Principle

The ERP should be designed to augment SkuVault rather than replace it.

SalesCore's competitive advantage lies in business operations, customer intelligence, reporting, automation, CRM, pricing, and fulfillment orchestration—not in rebuilding mature warehouse execution features that SkuVault already provides.

However, the architecture must remain modular enough that, if the business eventually decides to replace SkuVault, the Warehouse Integration Layer can be swapped with a native SalesCore Warehouse Engine without requiring significant changes to CRM, POS, Reporting, Customer 360, Product 360, or other ERP domains.
