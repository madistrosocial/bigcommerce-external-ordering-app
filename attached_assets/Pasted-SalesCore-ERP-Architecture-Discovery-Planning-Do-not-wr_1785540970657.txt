SalesCore ERP Architecture Discovery & Planning

Do not write any code yet.

This task is an architecture and planning exercise only.

The SalesCore application has grown beyond its original scope. It started as a POS/CRM integration with BigCommerce, but the long-term goal is to become the primary ERP system for our wholesale distribution business.

The current database evolved feature by feature and was not originally designed around a complete ERP architecture. Before implementing additional major modules, I want to evaluate the existing system and design a long-term architecture that minimizes redundancy, scales well, and provides a cohesive foundation.
