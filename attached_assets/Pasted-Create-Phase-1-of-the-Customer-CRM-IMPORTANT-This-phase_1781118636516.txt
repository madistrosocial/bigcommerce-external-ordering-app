Create Phase 1 of the Customer CRM.

IMPORTANT:
This phase is ONLY for building the Customer Mirror, Order Mirror, Customer Profile foundation, and Customer Export functionality.

Do NOT build:
- Customer Timeline
- Store Credits
- Claims Tracking
- Customer Intelligence
- Pricing Intelligence
- Zoho Sync
- Commission Tracking

Those will be implemented in later phases.

====================================
PHASE 1 GOAL
====================================

Create a Customer CRM foundation that mirrors BigCommerce customers and orders into the app database so future CRM features can be built without repeatedly querying BigCommerce.

This should become the central customer record for future CRM development.
