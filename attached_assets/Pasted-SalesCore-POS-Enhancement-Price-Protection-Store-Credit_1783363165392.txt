SalesCore POS Enhancement
Price Protection + Store Credit + Audit Reporting
Objective

Implement four related POS enhancements as a single checkout improvement:

Below Cost Price Protection
Store Credit Display
Store Credit Usage
Audit Reporting

This update extends the existing POS only. Do not rewrite or replace the checkout flow.

IMPORTANT

This update touches the POS.

After implementation:

Do not implement any new feature anywhere in the application until the POS completes full checkout UAT.

1. BELOW COST PRICE PROTECTION
Trigger

Validate whenever the cashier manually edits the Price ($) field.

Compare:

Manual Price
Product Cost (BigCommerce Cost field only)

Do not compare against selling price, MSRP, customer price or sale price.

Rules

If:

Manual Price >= Product Cost

Proceed normally.

If:

Manual Price < Product Cost

Display a confirmation dialog.

Warning Dialog

Title

⚠ Price Below Product Cost

Display:

Product
SKU
Product Cost
Entered Price
Difference

Message:

This sale will generate a loss. Do you want to continue?

Buttons:

No
Yes, Continue
No
Restore previous valid price
Clear manual override
Return focus to Price field
No audit log
Yes
Accept override
Continue existing checkout flow
Historical pricing continues to save the selling price
Create audit log
2. STORE CREDIT DISPLAY

Use the exact same Store Credit source used by the CRM Customer Profile.

The displayed balance must always match CRM.

POS Header

Display before the Wholesale button:

Store Credit: $68.40

If no customer:

Store Credit: $0.00
Discount Section

Replace the static value with the customer's actual available Store Credit.

3. STORE CREDIT USAGE

Allow cashiers to apply:

Full Store Credit
Partial Store Credit

Validation:

Applied Store Credit cannot exceed:

Available Store Credit
Order Total

Display checkout summary as:

Subtotal
Store Credit
Discount
Tax
Total

Store Credit must remain separate from Discounts.

Reuse BigCommerce's Store Credit implementation.

Do not create a custom Store Credit system.

After successful checkout automatically refresh Store Credit in:

Header
Discount panel
Customer summary

without page refresh.
