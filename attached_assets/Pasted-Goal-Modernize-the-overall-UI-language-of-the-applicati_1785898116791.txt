CRM Customer List Redesign

The goal is to increase information density while making the page cleaner and easier to scan.

Instead of separate columns for:

Company
Customer Name
Email

combine these into a single Customer column.

Each row should display:

Customer thumbnail
(logo if available, otherwise company initials)

Company Name
Primary Contact
Email Address

Example

○

Smokerz Inc.
John Smith
john@smokerz.com

This should become the standard list presentation for Customer CRM.

Do not display unnecessary information that already exists in the Customer Profile page.

Design Direction

Follow a modern enterprise ERP style.

Inspirations:

- Stripe Dashboard
- Linear
- Attio CRM
- Vercel Dashboard

The goal is not to copy these products, but to adopt their design principles:

clean typography

consistent spacing

high information density

minimal visual clutter

professional enterprise appearance
