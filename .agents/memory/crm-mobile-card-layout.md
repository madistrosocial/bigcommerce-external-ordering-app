---
name: CRM mobile customer cards
description: Mobile layout constraints for CRM customer cards, including business names and assigned reps.
---

On mobile CRM customer cards, give the business name a full-width header row above the two-column details. Align the reps row to the card's left content edge, but keep it within the left details column so it does not extend beneath the revenue/actions column. Keep health and order badges below the reps in that same left column.

**Why:** A full-card-width reps row crowded the right-side order summary and made the card look unbalanced.

**How to apply:** Preserve the two-column detail grid for identity and revenue. Place company identity above it, then put reps and health badges in the left grid column with no avatar indentation.
