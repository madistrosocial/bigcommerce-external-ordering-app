---
name: Product 360 editor presentation
description: User-approved organization and photo/category interactions for Product 360 catalog details.
---

In Product 360 product details, keep pricing and cost visually separate from physical specifications. Show assigned categories in a searchable, expandable checkbox tree rather than a flat checklist. At the top of Details, place the photo gallery on the left at roughly 65% width and categories on the right; use 150px photo tiles with compact, editable captions and make the upload target an empty gallery tile. In Variants, show each variant's thumbnail in the first column and let admins upload a missing image directly to that variant.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested and approved these presentation and interaction choices while refining the product details editor. BigCommerce has a separate variant-image endpoint; product-level images do not guarantee a particular image is assigned to that variant.

**How to apply:** Preserve these patterns when extending or restyling Product 360. Keep variant uploads scoped to the selected BigCommerce variant.
