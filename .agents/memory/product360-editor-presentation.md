---
name: Product 360 editor presentation
description: User-approved organization and photo/category interactions for Product 360 catalog details.
---

In Product 360 product details, keep pricing and cost visually separate from physical specifications. Show assigned categories in a searchable, expandable checkbox tree rather than a flat checklist. At the top of Details, place the photo gallery on the left at roughly 65% width and categories on the right; use 150px photo tiles with compact, editable captions and make the upload target an empty gallery tile.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested and approved these presentation and interaction choices while refining the product details editor.

**How to apply:** Preserve these patterns when extending or restyling the Product 360 Details tab.
